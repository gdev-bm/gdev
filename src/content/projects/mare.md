Mare is a solo colony game with an isometric camera, in the spirit of Factorio and RimWorld. You arrive on the Moon aboard a Starship with a few supplies and one mining robot, and build outwards from there.

By day it is an automation game. You build solar arrays and batteries, paint habitats and greenhouses square by square, mine regolith, and run it along conveyor belts through processors, fabricators and assemblers. Robots take over the repetitive work: rovers mine, haulers deliver, androids build and defend. Satellites you launch earn research that unlocks the rest of the tech tree, and eventually the voyage to Mars.

By night it turns into horror. The caves under the surface are dark, deep and full of things that hunt by your headlamp. Raids come up from below after dark, and the base needs turrets, fences and androids to survive them.

Everything runs in the browser in Three.js, with no build step. The models are built in Blender, mostly by Python scripts.
